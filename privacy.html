Privacy Policy

PipeMark  ·  Effective date: October 4, 2026

In short: PipeMark does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

1. Information we collect

PipeMark does not collect any personal information. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, photo library, microphone, camera, or any other personal data in order to function.

“Collect” means transmitting data off your device to us or to any third party. PipeMark does neither.

2. Data stored on your device

3. Third-party services

PipeMark does not integrate any third-party analytics, attribution, crash reporting, advertising, or social SDKs. The app relies only on Apple-provided system frameworks. Any data handled by Apple system services is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

4. Children’s privacy

PipeMark is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

5. Changes to this policy

If PipeMark’s data practices change, we will update this page and revise the effective date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
