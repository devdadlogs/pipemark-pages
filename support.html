Frequently asked

Does PipeMark require an account?

No. PipeMark works entirely on your device. There is no sign-up, no login, and no account to manage.

Does PipeMark collect my data?

No. See our Privacy Policy for details. Everything stays on your device.

How do I delete everything PipeMark has saved?

Delete the app from your iPhone or iPad. Because all data is stored locally, removing the app removes all of its data.
